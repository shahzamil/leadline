QUESTIONS

Simple Answers

Do I pay for the ads?+

No. We pay for all ads. You only pay for real calls that reach you.

What is a "real" call?+

A call from a person who needs your service, in your area, that lasts 90 seconds or more. Shorter calls, wrong numbers and spam are free.

When do I pay?+

Once a week, after the calls come in. We send you the list of calls first, then you pay.

How do I know the calls are real?+

Every call goes through a tracking number. You see the time, length and caller number of each call. If one looks wrong, we check it together.

Is there a long contract?+

No. Try it. If it does not work for you, you can stop.
